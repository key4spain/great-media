English

When you submit our application form, we collect your name, phone number, email address and your answers (location, vehicle, availability). We use this information solely to contact you about courier work opportunities, usually via WhatsApp, and to guide you through the onboarding process. We do not sell or share your data with third parties outside of our recruitment process. You can request deletion of your data at any time by messaging us.
